## SingularityNET (AGIX) Fundamental Analysis

Understanding why AGIX was a compelling Layer 4 candidate — and why the merger outcome matters:

### What AGIX Was

SingularityNET was founded by Ben Goertzel (AGI researcher) and Cassio Pennachin in 2017. The protocol created a decentralized marketplace where AI developers could publish, discover, and monetize AI models and services as composable primitives. Key properties:

- **Token function:** Pay-for-access to AI model APIs, governance voting
- **Architecture:** Ethereum + Cardano dual-chain (AGIX was cross-chain native)
- **Narrative:** The "app store for AI" — tokenized ML service economy
- **Differentiation from FET:** FET agents *execute tasks*; AGIX agents *sell capabilities*
- **Data marketplace link:** OCEAN provided the data; AGIX monetized the models; FET orchestrated agents

### Why FR Differential Hypothesis Was Valid Pre-Merger

Had AGIX remained independent, the FR driver hypothesis would have been:

1. **AI model release cycles:** ChatGPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Gemini 2.0 launches → AGIX hype spikes → positive FR
2. **SingularityNET milestones:** New AI service listings, Ben Goertzel AGI statements → community sentiment → FR premium
3. **ASI Alliance activity:** Pre-merger governance votes, cross-chain bridge events → volume → FR volatility
4. **AI regulation:** EU AI Act, US executive orders on AI capability thresholds → risk-off → FR swings

Expected vol ratio vs BTC: 2.5–4.0x (smaller MC, narrative-driven, AI hype amplifier)

### Why FR Signal Is Now Consolidated into FET

Post-merger, all AGIX buying pressure (from holders seeking AI marketplace exposure) now flows into FET. The SingularityNET community's speculative demand, which would have driven AGIX FR, is now reflected in FET-PERP funding rates. This is exactly what K546 captured: FET Sh=40.06 (six months OOS), incorporating the merged narrative.

The merger was a net positive for FET FR signal strength — three distinct AI sub-narratives compressed into one ticker means higher FR volatility when any sub-narrative triggers. However, this also means higher correlation with broad AI sentiment, contributing to the G5 cluster block observed in K546.
